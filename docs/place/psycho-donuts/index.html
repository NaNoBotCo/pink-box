a shop · CAPsycho Donuts

A Campbell, California shop themed on "craziness", with donut names punning on mental illness, a straitjacket on the wall and a padded cell in the room.

When they’re open

open closed not published · Cited · osm · why so many close Sunday

The story Cited · wp-psycho-donuts

Jordan Zweigoron and Kipp Berdianski opened it in March 2009; Berdiansky left the partnership that July. A San Jose location and a Santa Clara location have both closed, as has a 2021 pop-up at Valley Fair.

The theme has drawn objection from mental-health advocates, which is part of the shop's public record rather than an aside to it.

Today Cited · wp-psycho-donuts
