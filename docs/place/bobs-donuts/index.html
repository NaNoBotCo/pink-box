The story Cited · wp-bob-s-donuts

The shop opened at 1621 Polk Street in 1953 or 1960 — accounts differ — and the original owner is not firmly established, though a city report points to Robert N. Nolt, who ran several businesses on the street. Elinor and Ronald Ahn bought it in 1977. After Elinor died in 2001 it passed to her son and daughter-in-law; Aya Ahn is a co-owner.

Around 2012 the shop introduced the Bob's Donut Challenge: eat a giant donut the size of twelve regular ones in under three minutes, and your name goes on a wall.
