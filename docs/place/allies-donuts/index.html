The story Cited · wp-allie-s-donuts

Frederick Alvin "Allie" Briggs opened Allie's Hand-Cut Do-nut Shop on 1 June 1968 in a former sub sandwich shop, with about forty dollars in hand and a seven-thousand-dollar debt. He was a dairy farmer from Point Judith with ten years of donut experience from a shop in Wakefield. He took $38 on the first day, selling close to a hundred dozen at 39 cents each.

In 1969 his son Ricky was killed by a drunk driver while out delivering donuts for the business alongside his cousin. Briggs nearly closed.

Today Cited · wp-allie-s-donuts

Customers drive in from Connecticut and Massachusetts. Saveur listed it among the country's best donut shops in 2008; Thrillist did the same in 2016.
