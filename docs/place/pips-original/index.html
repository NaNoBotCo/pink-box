A northeast Portland shop frying small cake donuts to order and serving house chai beside them.

The story Cited · wp-pip-s-original-doughnuts-chai

The shop is on Fremont Street in northeast Portland; Nate Snell is a co-owner. A second location in Beaverton ran through 2023 and 2024. Vogue has described an always-out-the-door line at the Portland shop.

Today Inference

Made-to-order is the opposite end of the trade from a case filled at four in the morning, and costs accordingly.
