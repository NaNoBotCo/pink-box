The story Cited · wp-federal-donuts

The founders took the idea from Cafe Soho in Philadelphia, which serves Korean fried chicken. The first shop opened in Pennsport in 2011; by 2018 there were six, plus stands at Citizens Bank Park. A Miami branch opened in 2017 and closed in 2018. In 2024 the flagship moved from 1219 South 2nd Street to 22 Wolf Street in South Philadelphia. Michael Solomonov owns the chain.
