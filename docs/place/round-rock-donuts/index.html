The story Cited · wp-round-rock-donuts

Established in 1926 as Lone Star Bakery, it moved to 106 West Liberty in 1970 because the old spot at 101 East Main had nowhere to park. A second Austin location opened on Burnet Road in January 1977. A second modern location opened in Cedar Park in 2022.

Today Cited · wp-round-rock-donuts

It has turned up on best-of lists for fifty years: Texas Monthly in 1976, Bon Appétit in 2010, The Daily Meal in 2013, Yelp in 2022, TasteAtlas in 2025.
