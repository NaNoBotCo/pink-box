on the side · TXKlobasnek

also: klobásník · klobasniky · Texas kolache (as sold)

Ask a Czech Texan and you get the distinction; ask anyone else and you get a sausage roll.

Sausage — klobása, traditionally — wrapped in kolache dough and baked. Ham, egg, cheese, jalapeño and hot dog are all standard now, and in some Texas shops boudin, the Cajun pork-and-rice sausage.

The story Cited · wp-klob-sn-k

Unlike the kolache, which came with Moravian immigrants, the klobasnek was invented by Czechs who had already settled in Texas. Texans of Czech ancestry call the savoury doughs klobasniky; Texans who are not call them kolaches, and that is what the sign says. They are sold everywhere from gas stations to dedicated kolache shops, well outside the Czech counties.
