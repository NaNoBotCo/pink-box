a donutBavarian cream

also: custard-filled · cream-filled · Boston cream (when chocolate-topped)

Ask whether it is custard or whipped. The answer tells you what kind of shop you are in.

A raised round with no hole, filled with a set custard — Bavarian cream is custard lightened with whipped cream and held with gelatin — and finished in sugar, or iced with chocolate, at which point most counters call it a Boston cream.

The story Cited · wp-bavarian-cream

The filled fried round is the older European form; the American shop split it by filling. Jam is a jelly donut or a bismarck; pastry cream and its relatives are custard, Bavarian or Boston cream depending on the shop and the region. The words are not standard and no authority governs them.

How it is done Cited · wp-bavarian-cream

Fry the round, cool it, then inject from the side with a nozzle until it gains weight in the hand. Chocolate icing goes on the top face after.

Today Tradition

The filled donuts are the ones a shop fills in the morning and the ones left over at closing, because a filled donut does not hold overnight the way a glazed ring does.
