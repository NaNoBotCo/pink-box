also: 24-hour donut shop · the late window

Two in the morning is a different customer and the same donut.

A shop that never closes, or opens again at midnight: bar traffic, night shifts, students, and whoever else is out.

The story Cited · wp-bob-s-donuts

Bob's Donuts on Polk Street in San Francisco has run 24 hours and draws a nightlife crowd. Seaside Donuts Bakery in Newport Beach has been open around the clock since 1985. Randy's original drive-in in Inglewood is a 24-hour window.

The all-night shop is only possible because the fryer runs at night anyway — the overnight bake and the overnight trade are the same shift seen from two sides.

Today Inference

Where a city has a late counter, it tends to be one of the few places open at that hour that is not a bar or a hospital.
